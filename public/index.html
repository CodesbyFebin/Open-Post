Get started

  1. Add a channel in Channels. Use sandbox to try things with nothing leaving your machine.
  2. For real networks, set the env vars shown on the channel card (e.g. X_API_KEY) and restart.
  3. Write a post in Compose, or let Autopilot fill your cadence slots.
